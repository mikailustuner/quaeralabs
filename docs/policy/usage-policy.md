# Usage policy

**Version:** 1.0 · Approved at the Phase 0 gate on 2026-10-03.

QuaeraLabs is an open-source tool designed for the advancement of science. Everyone who uses the tool, and especially everyone who publishes its results, accepts this policy. Publishing to the shared network (v1.1) depends on accepting this policy.

## What not to do

1. **Present AI output as human work.** Research produced by the agent team may not be published with the QuaeraLabs AI label removed or hidden.
2. **Mass-produce fake papers.** Using the tool to generate many papers that have not gone through human review and submit them to journals, conferences or preprint servers is forbidden.
3. **Fabricated data or results.** Editing run records, metrics or Lean output by hand and presenting them as verified.
4. **Harmful research.** v1.0 covers only mathematics and AI/ML. Using the tool for research aimed at biological, chemical, nuclear or radiological weapons, attacks on critical infrastructure, or harming people is forbidden.
5. **Unauthorized data.** Using data whose license does not permit it, or personal data without its owners' consent.

## What to do

- Leave the limitations of every published result and any deviations from the preregistration in the report.
- Read and approve the report before publishing a result; approval means taking responsibility for the result.
- When an error is discovered, retract the publication or publish a corrected revision.

## Violations

Publications on the shared network found to be in violation are removed and the account may be suspended. The process is run through the moderation and appeal layers on the roadmap.
